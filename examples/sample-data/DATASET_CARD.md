# MOMENT v1 Tutorial Samples

## Summary

These are **original synthetic, deterministic teaching datasets** for the MOMENT DIMER pipeline. They are generated by [`generate_samples.py`](generate_samples.py) from fixed mathematical formulas—no random sampling, copied benchmark rows, personal data, or third-party dataset content.

The generator produces three tutorial-facing artifacts:

| Artifact | Rows | Series | Channels | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|---|
| `moment_clean.csv` | 512 | 1 | `vibration`, `temperature` | 15 minutes | embeddings and artificial-mask imputation |
| `moment_anomaly.csv` | 512 | 1 | same | 15 minutes | raw reconstruction-residual anomaly scoring |
| `moment_anomaly_labels.csv` | 256 | 1 | label table | 15 minutes | identifies three synthetic injected spikes for ranking/visualization only |

Each signal contains 256 timestamps. MOMENT's canonical converter therefore left-pads the sequence to the pinned 512-step model length and records that padding explicitly.

## Clean signals

For `t = 0..255` at 15-minute intervals:

```text
vibration = 1.5 + 0.0008*t
          + 0.35*sin(2*pi*t/32)
          + 0.08*cos(2*pi*t/16)

temperature = 28 + 0.0015*t
            + 2.5*sin(2*pi*(t+9)/96)
            + 0.08*cos(2*pi*t/16)
```

The two channels deliberately have different scales and periodicities so the multichannel examples are visually distinguishable. They are not meant to approximate a particular machine, sensor, or physical process.

## Artificial imputation evaluation

The imputation tutorial starts from `moment_clean.csv` and deliberately hides complete 8-step patches. Ground-truth values remain available only for tutorial scoring; the mask passed to MOMENT marks those positions unobserved. The reported MAE/RMSE is computed **only on deliberately hidden source-observed points**. Source-missing values, if a BYOD file contains them, have no ground truth and are excluded.

## Injected anomaly sample

`moment_anomaly.csv` copies the clean series and modifies the `vibration` channel at three timestamps (indices 184, 201, and 233) by fixed deltas `+2.8`, `-3.2`, and `+3.6`. The labels file marks only these injected points.

The labels are provided to demonstrate whether known injections rank highly under the raw reconstruction residual. They are **not used to fit or calibrate a threshold**, and the tutorial does not turn MOMENT's raw score into a universal binary anomaly classifier.

## Preprocessing

No interpolation, scaling, filtering, learned normalization, or stochastic augmentation is performed by the generator. Values are serialized with six decimal places. MOMENT's own RevIN remains part of the model execution path.

## Integrity

[`SHA256SUMS`](SHA256SUMS) records canonical UTF-8 CSV bytes generated with:

- timestamps formatted as `%Y-%m-%dT%H:%M:%S`;
- floats formatted as `%.6f`;
- LF newlines.

The unit suite regenerates the files in a temporary directory and checks the recorded SHA-256 values and expected schemas. Tutorial notebooks invoke the same generator, so their sample provenance is executable rather than an undocumented download.

## Intended use and limits

Use these artifacts for:

- live/tutorial execution;
- BYOD schema reference;
- embedding export examples;
- patch-mask imputation evaluation;
- raw anomaly-score visualization/ranking;
- CI smoke evidence.

Do **not** use them to claim real-world accuracy, calibration, robustness, anomaly-detection performance, or superiority over another model. Their structure is intentionally pedagogical and may be much easier than operational time-series data.
